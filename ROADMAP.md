# Roadmap

PortraitLocal `0.4.0` is the current bounded MVP. The items below are future
candidates, not implemented features or promises of a scheduled release.

## Future Candidates

- RAW development workflows;
- photographic styles and reusable looks;
- generative or interactive edit modes;
- dedicated denoise, deblur or artifact-repair model families;
- queue cancellation, retry and progress controls;
- series consistency and automatic photo selection;
- composition analysis and broader scene coverage;
- additional provider and model families.

Future work must preserve the local-first workflow, original-image safety and
the rule that generative processing never silently replaces ordinary
enhancement.
